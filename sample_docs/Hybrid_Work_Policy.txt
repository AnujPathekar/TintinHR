TintinHR Hybrid Work Policy — Effective 1 April 2026

Eligible Bengaluru employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Tuesday and Thursday are the standard collaboration days in the office for Engineering. Teams may define additional anchor days based on customer or operational needs.

Employees must work from India unless written approval for temporary international work has been granted by People Operations and Information Security. Company information must only be accessed on managed devices using the approved VPN. Confidential conversations must not be conducted in public areas.

The policy does not create a permanent contractual right to remote work. A manager may temporarily require office attendance for onboarding, incident response, performance support or customer meetings. Accessibility and medical accommodations follow a separate confidential process managed by People Operations.
