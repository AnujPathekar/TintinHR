TintinHR Leave Policy — Effective 1 January 2026

1. Casual Leave
Employees receive 12 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave is credited monthly. A maximum of 3 consecutive casual-leave days may be taken without additional HR approval. Unused casual leave expires on 31 December and cannot be carried forward.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate may be requested when sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive working days. Up to 5 unused sick-leave days may be carried into the next calendar year, subject to a maximum accumulated balance of 15 days.

3. Probation
Employees on probation accrue casual and sick leave from their joining date. They may use accrued leave after completing 30 calendar days. Any exception requires manager and HR approval.

4. Applying for Leave
Planned leave should be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance. Emergency leave should be reported to the manager as soon as reasonably possible. A submitted request remains pending until approved and is deducted from the available balance while pending.

5. Policy Questions
The employee's current balance is maintained in the HR portal. The policy describes eligibility; it does not itself prove an individual employee's current balance. Contact People Operations when an exception or conflict requires confirmation.
